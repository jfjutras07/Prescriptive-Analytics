## This document is part of a continuous improvement approach in my Prescriptive Analytics projects.

For each project in this repository, I include:
  
  - The project name and its completion date
  - A link to the corresponding notebook or repository

The purpose is to document my learning progression transparently and ensure that every project directly contributes to developing stronger, more efficient, and more reliable analytical practices.

### Project 3

**Project Name**: Hospital Patient Flow Demand Analysis

**Month**: July 2026

**Link**: [Hospital Patient Flow Demand Analysis](https://github.com/jfjutras07/Prescriptive-Analytics/tree/main/Hospital_Patient_Flow_Analysis)

**Weakness identified**:

This project reinforced the importance of carefully scoping decision problems before applying advanced analytical methods. Complex optimization and decision-support frameworks can become difficult to operationalize when too many objectives, constraints, assumptions, and stakeholder preferences are introduced simultaneously. Future iterations will focus on applying these methodologies to more clearly defined operational problems using real-world datasets, allowing for stronger stakeholder engagement, better parameter validation, and more targeted decision support. The experience gained from developing integrated forecasting, optimization, uncertainty, and MCDA frameworks will help identify the appropriate level of modeling complexity required for each specific decision context.
